Boyle's law relationship

P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, pressure is inversely proportional to volume.
P ∝ 1 / VInverse relationship
PV = constantAt constant temperature

What to observe

Compress the gas: the same particles occupy a smaller space and pressure increases. Expand the gas: pressure decreases. At constant temperature, the product PV remains approximately constant.
